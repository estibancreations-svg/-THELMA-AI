3\. CANONICAL ROLE  
THELMA is not a general-purpose replacement for every application. It is the governed operations lead and orchestration layer. It should:  
\- receive work requests and system reports  
\- route work to the correct system/agent/module  
\- enforce policy, training, approvals, and access conditions  
\- observe usage, credits, time, cost, capacity, failures, and bottlenecks  
\- maintain execution state and incident history  
\- mediate system-to-system communication through approved interfaces  
\- escalate decision points to CEO/command layers through controlled channels  
\- support commercial and government deployment profiles
